# Privacy notice of this gateway

This notice applies to this Baylee gateway. Your Baylee Central account is
covered by Central's [privacy policy](https://baylee.app/privacy).

*Template text of the Baylee project. It describes what the gateway software
processes out of the box. Each operator adapts the parts in square brackets
and adds what they do on top. It is not legal advice.*

## 2. What the gateway processes

- **Sign-in:** your Baylee Central account ID, your username and display
  name, sessions (stored as check values) and short-lived tickets. Your
  password never reaches the gateway. *Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR*
- **Playing:** tables and the lobby (in memory), your decks and their
  versions, your settings, uploaded sleeves and playmats. *Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR*
- **Friends and chat:** friendships, requests, blocks, groups, your devices'
  public keys and your chat settings. Messages to friends, groups and tables
  are end-to-end encrypted; the gateway holds messages not yet delivered in
  encrypted form and cannot read them. The lobby chat is public and not
  encrypted: the gateway passes it to everyone connected and keeps the last
  50 messages in memory until it restarts. *Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR*
- **Game records:** every game played here with all actions and cards,
  beside it which account sat in which seat and whether you show your hidden
  cards in replays. For replays and finding bugs. *Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR*
- **Acceptance of the terms:** which version you accepted and when; the
  record is kept in your Baylee Central account. *Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR*
- **Reports and moderation:** reported content (for a reported chat message,
  its text, which your device sends along), decisions and suspensions.
  *Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR*
- **Bug reports** you send, with the attachments you leave ticked; the
  gateway passes them on to [the service the operator sends bug reports
  to]. They stay linked to your account through a pseudonym so the operator
  can follow up and answer you; a screenshot shows what your window showed,
  names and chat at the table included. They serve only to find bugs and to
  answer you. *Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR* (a game without bugs)
- **Operation and moderation:** administrators see who is playing, at which
  table and with whom, and an account's recent games. *Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR*
  (secure and fair operation)
- **Security:** IP addresses and entered sign-in names briefly in memory to
  slow down abuse, and server logs. *Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR* (secure operation)

- **Training the game's AI and balancing:** records, anonymised: without
  account, game ID or time. [Delete this point if you do not use records
  for it; name any further purpose with its legal basis and how to object.]
  *Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR* (a better computer opponent)

## 3. Recipients

- **Hosting:** [provider and location; as a processor].
- **Baylee Central** confirms your sign-in.
- **[The bug report service]** receives the reports you send.
- **Other players** see what the game shows them: your display name, the
  cards you play openly, your sleeves and playmats.

## 4. How long data is kept

- Sessions: 12 hours without use; tickets: seconds to minutes.
- Decks, settings, friends, groups: until you delete them or your account.
- Messages not yet delivered: until delivery, at most [14] days.
- Sleeves and playmats: as long as an account or a deck uses them.
- Game records: [duration]. If you delete your account, the link to you is
  removed.
- Server logs: [duration].
- Backups: [duration].

## 5. Your rights

You have the rights under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR (access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, portability, objection) and may lodge a complaint with
a data protection supervisory authority. Much of this you can do yourself in
the settings; otherwise a message to the contact details in the gateway's
legal notice is enough.
