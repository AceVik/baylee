# Terms of use of this gateway

This Baylee gateway is run by the person or organisation named in the
gateway's legal notice (the "operator"). These terms apply to playing and
chatting on this gateway. Your Baylee account is also subject to the terms
of use of Baylee Central.

*This is a translation for your convenience. Only the German version is
legally binding.*

## 1. What this gateway offers

On this gateway you can use your Baylee account to open tables, play against
other people and computer opponents, watch, save decks and chat with
friends. Use is free of charge. There is no entitlement to any particular
availability; games may end early because of maintenance or faults.

## 2. Fair play and respect

You agree

- to play fair: no arrangements to others' disadvantage, no exploiting bugs,
  no tools that show you hidden information or play for you, unless the
  gateway offers them itself;
- not to insult, threaten, harass or discriminate against others, neither in
  chat nor through names, deck names or images;
- not to upload or spread unlawful content or infringe anyone's rights;
- not to overload or attack the gateway, and to connect only with the
  official Baylee clients.

## 3. Chat and end-to-end encryption

- Messages to friends and to your table are encrypted on your device and
  decrypted only on the recipients' devices. The gateway passes them on but
  **cannot read their content**.
- The gateway sees only the circumstances: who sends a message of what size
  to whom, and when. It keeps messages not yet delivered, encrypted, for at
  most 30 days.
- **If you report a message**, your device sends its content to this
  gateway's moderators so they can review the report. Only then does anyone
  besides the people in the conversation see a message's content.
- The **lobby chat** is public and **not encrypted**: everyone connected
  to this gateway at the time reads it, and the gateway can read its
  content. It keeps the last 50 messages in memory until it restarts and
  does not store them permanently. Write nothing there that not everyone
  should read.
- Computer opponents and spectators take no part in chat.

## 4. Your content

You are responsible for decks, deck names, uploaded images (such as sleeves
and playmats) and messages. You allow the operator to store them for running
the gateway and to show them to other players as the game intends. You keep
all rights to them. Upload only what you may use.

## 5. Game records and replays

1. Every game played on this gateway is recorded in full: every action and
   every card of every player, hidden cards included. The records let you
   watch your games again as replays and let bugs be traced.
2. This gateway's software does not delete records by itself. Which account
   sat in which seat is stored beside them; if you delete your account, that
   link is removed. How long the operator keeps records is stated in its
   privacy notice.
3. You can watch the games you took part in again; so can the other players
   of the same game. Whether they see your hidden cards is your choice, per
   game, at the end of the game or later in your list of replays. For new
   games it is on until you switch it off.
4. The operator may use records anonymised, that is without account, game
   ID or time, to train the game's AI and to balance decks. It neither sells
   them nor passes them on.

## 6. Bug reports, reports and moderation

1. You send a bug report with the client's report function. It stays
   linked to your account (through a pseudonym) so the operator can follow
   it up and later contact you about it. It carries what you send along:
   your text and, if ticked, a screenshot of the client window (which can
   show names and chat at the table), system details, your game log and
   the game's record. The operator uses bug reports only to find and fix
   bugs and to answer you, not for training and not to pass on.
2. You can report breaches or unlawful content with the report function in the
   client or through the contact details in the gateway's legal notice. The
   operator reviews reports promptly. For breaches the operator may remove
   content, exclude you from tables or suspend your account on this gateway,
   temporarily or permanently, to an extent proportionate to the breach, and
   tells you why and how you can object. A suspension on this gateway does
   not affect your Baylee account on other gateways.
