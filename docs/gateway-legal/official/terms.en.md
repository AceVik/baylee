- **Administrators**: me and the people I entrust with operation, bug fixing or moderation and bind to confidentiality.
- **Account**: a registered user account. **Guest account**: an account without a username and password.
- **Beta key**: an invitation key without which no new account and no new guest account can be created during a closed beta.
- **Table**: a room in the lobby where a game is prepared and played. **Seat**: a place at a table. **Spectator**: someone who follows a running game without taking part in it.
- **House AI**: the computer opponents and stand-ins the gateway itself provides.
- **AI seat** (also language-model seat): a seat played by a language model. **Seat bridge**: the helper program that connects an AI seat to the language model; except for hosted AI seats it runs on your device. **Hosted AI seat**: an AI seat whose seat bridge and model access I operate (section 8.10).
- **Chair ticket**: a short-lived single-use key with which a seat bridge takes a seat at a table. **Seat token** and **socket ticket**: keys that open a game, lobby, spectator or chat connection.
- **Record**: the complete log of a game (section 9). **Replay**: a view of a game computed afresh from the record (section 9.7). **Report**: a bug, crash or idea report, or a reported chat message (section 10).
- **Chat**: friends, groups and messages under section 9a. **Device** (in chat): an installation of the client that has its own chat keys for your account.
- **Content**: anything you yourself place on the gateway or send through it, such as names, group names, decks, notes, pictures, chat messages and report texts.

**9a.2 End-to-end encryption.** The content of every message except the lobby chat's (text, card links, mentions) is encrypted on the sender's device for the recipients' devices and decrypted only there. The gateway receives and stores only encrypted content and has no key that opens it; I therefore can neither read nor hand over the content of your messages, except a message someone reports (section 10.8). This holds as long as the devices a conversation is encrypted for really belong to the people in it: the gateway keeps the directory of device keys, and a manipulated server could add a foreign device and read new messages. Your client therefore shows every new device of the people you talk to and of your own account, and with the safety number you check that you are writing to the right devices. A contact is "Verified" only if you mark it so yourself after comparing.

**9a.3 What I see nonetheless.** Only the content is encrypted. The gateway sees and stores who is friends with whom, requests and blocks, the name, members and open invitations of each group, and each account's devices (random identifier, public keys, operating-system family, when set up and when last connected). For each message it stores, until delivery, which account sent it from which device to which conversation, when, and how large it is. In memory it holds who is online, with which status and at which table. From this it can be seen who writes to whom and when. I write none of this to any log. Whoever may write to you sees the public keys of your devices with their operating-system family and set-up date.

**9a.4 Storage and deletion.** The gateway keeps a message, encrypted, only until every recipient device has fetched it, for at most 14 days, for a guest's device 1 day. It stores no history; the history is kept encrypted on your devices, 30 days by default (adjustable under "Keep chat history on this device"). Delivered messages stay on the recipients' devices, even if you delete them on yours or delete your account. Backups may contain undelivered messages, still encrypted, for up to 12 months (section 16.3). A group is deleted by its owner; an empty group is deleted.

**9a.5 Your devices.** A device's chat keys are kept in its operating system's credential store or, where there is none, in a file only you can read. Anyone who can use your device unlocked can read the history on it and write in your name. If you lose a device, remove it from another one under Settings → Chat & privacy → Your devices and have your password reset (section 4.4). That does not protect older messages: anyone who obtains a device's keys can read every message to that device of which they hold an encrypted copy. Messages are signed by the sending device; a recipient can prove that a message came from your device (section 10.8). An account can have up to 5 devices, a guest one.

**9a.6 Guests.** Guests can write only at their table: in table and team chat and to individual players at their table, if those allow it. Friends, groups and invitations do not exist for guests. When a guest signs out, the client deletes their chat history and chat keys on that device.

**9a.7 Who receives messages.** Table and team messages go to the accounts seated at that table or in that team when they are sent, group messages to the members. Spectators, the house AI and AI seats, hosted ones included, receive none. Chat never enters game processes, records, replays, the training of the house AI or reports, except a reported message (section 10.8).
